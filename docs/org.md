## What we do

- **Content:** weekly YouTube videos plus regular live streams over lunchtime on weekdays.
- **Online Local:** weekly online tournament every Sunday night — four rounds of SWU in regular Swiss format, run on [melee.gg](https://melee.gg).
- **Leaderboard:** points awarded to top finishers across Online Locals, tracked manually in Google Sheets today. Automating this is the main reason for the new site.
- **Prize wall:** players redeem accumulated points for prizes.
